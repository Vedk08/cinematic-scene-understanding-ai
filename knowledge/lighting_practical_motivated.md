Motivated lighting appears to come from a source visible or implied within the scene — a window, lamp, fire, television, or street light — so the illumination feels natural and grounded in the world. Practical lights are functioning light sources actually within the frame. These choices increase realism and can localize mood: a single warm practical in a dark room creates an intimate pool of light; flickering or colored practicals (neon, screens, fire) tint the scene and signal environment and time. Unmotivated, stylized lighting (light with no clear in-world source) announces artifice and authorial control. A bright concentrated source against an otherwise dark frame suggests a motivated key or a narrow, story-driven light.
