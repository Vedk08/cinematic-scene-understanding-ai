Blocking is the arrangement and movement of subjects within the frame and the space. Single-subject blocking concentrates the scene on one figure's presence and interiority. Two-person staging encodes relationship through distance and arrangement: close spacing reads as intimacy, conspiracy, or tension; wide separation reads as estrangement, conflict, or power imbalance. Stacking subjects in depth (one near, one far) creates foreground-background hierarchy and can show dominance or dependence. Visual weight — who occupies more frame area or sits at a stronger compositional point — signals who holds power in the moment. Staging that places characters on the same depth plane and similar size suggests equality, while strong size disparity suggests imbalance.
