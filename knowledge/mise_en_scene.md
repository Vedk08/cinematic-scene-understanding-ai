Mise-en-scene ("placing on stage") is everything arranged within the frame: setting, set decoration, props, costume, lighting, and the staging of figures. A dense, cluttered environment can express chaos, wealth, history, or psychological overload; a sparse, minimal environment isolates the subject and abstracts the moment. The relationship between subject and environment matters: when the environment dominates the frame, place and atmosphere carry the meaning; when the subject dominates, the surroundings recede to supporting detail. Detected objects function as production-design signals — domestic objects imply controlled interior space, vehicles and street furniture imply public exterior space — letting an analysis infer setting type and the subject's relationship to their world.
