Science fiction frequently uses cool palettes (blues, teals, steely grays) and clean, geometric, often symmetrical compositions to signal control, technology, sterility, or alienation. Hard or directional lighting and reflective surfaces emphasize machinery and artificial environments. Wide shots stress scale — vast interiors, monumental architecture, or empty landscapes that dwarf the human figure. Dystopian sci-fi may desaturate and harshen the look; hopeful or wondrous sci-fi may use brighter, cleaner light and lens flares for awe. When a frame combines a cool tone, controlled geometric composition, and a sense of scale or sterility, it leans on science-fiction conventions, used to evoke either technological wonder or unsettling detachment.
