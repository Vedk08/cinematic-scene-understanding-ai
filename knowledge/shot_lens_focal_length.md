Lens focal length changes how space and faces feel, independent of how close the camera is. Wide-angle lenses exaggerate depth and distance, can distort faces at close range, and make spaces feel larger or more dynamic; they suit immersion, unease, or grandeur. Telephoto (long) lenses compress space, flattening the distance between subject and background, isolating a subject and creating a voyeuristic or detached quality. A "normal" lens (around 50mm on full frame) approximates human perspective and feels natural. Compression or exaggerated depth in a frame is a strong stylistic signal even when the shot size is similar, so the sense of flattened or stretched space is worth reading alongside composition.
